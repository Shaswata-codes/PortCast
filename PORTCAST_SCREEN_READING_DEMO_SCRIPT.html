🎬 PortCast: “Just-Read-The-Screen” Live Demo Guide

You Don’t Need to Memorize Anything! Just follow this screen-by-screen reading cue card.

🧭 STEP 1: Open the App (#home or #dashboard)

🗣️ What to say:

“Good morning judges! Today I’m presenting PortCast—an AI freight intelligence platform that acts like Google Flights for multi-million dollar cargo ships bringing coal and iron ore into India.”

📊 STEP 2: Click “Overview” in Top Bar (#dashboard)

(Look at your screen and read the 4 big cards at the top from Left to Right)

1️⃣ Read Card 1 (Top-Left):

  • Point at: $18.40 / MT (Spot Freight Rate)
  • Say: “Here we see today’s live spot rate to ship cargo into India’s East Coast, with a 14-day green sparkline showing price momentum.”

2️⃣ Read Card 2:

  • Point at: $584.81 VLSFO (Bunker Fuel)
  • Say: “This is live marine fuel price. Fuel is 40% of a ship’s voyage cost.”

3️⃣ Read Card 3:

  • Point at: 34.4 / 100 (Risk Index)
  • Say: “Our AI monitors geopolitical friction in the Red Sea and Strait of Malacca.”

4️⃣ Read Bottom Table:

  • Point at: Paradip (1.8d), Vizag (1.2d), Haldia (3.4d)
  • Say: “Down here, we track live waiting times at East Coast ports so ships don’t get stuck in traffic queues.”

🧠 STEP 3: Click “Forecaster” in Top Bar (#forecaster)

(This is the main hero of your project!)

1️⃣ Read the Big Chart:

  • Point at the solid line: “This is the past 180 days of actual shipping prices.”
  • Point at the cyan line going forward: “This is our AI predicting prices for the next 30 days.”
  • Point at the shaded band: “The shaded corridor shows our P10 best-case floor to P90 worst-case ceiling.”

2️⃣ Read the 30-Day Heatmap Strip (Directly Under the Chart):

(This is super easy to explain!) - Point at Blue cells: “Blue cells = CHEAP DAYS to book.” - Point at Orange cells: “Orange cells = EXPENSIVE DAYS.” - Point at the Gold Star (★): “Look at this Gold Star (★) on Day +12. Our AI is telling the charterer: ‘Don’t book today—wait until Day +12 to lock in the lowest price and save $500,000!’”

3️⃣ Drag the Slider (Live Interactive Magic!):

  • Action: Grab the “Bunker Fuel Shock” slider and slide it to +20%.
  • Say: “Watch the chart: when oil prices spike by 20%, our model instantly lifts the entire 30-day forecast curve in real time!”

🚢 STEP 4: Click “Optimizer” in Top Bar (#optimizer)

1️⃣ Read the 4 Vessel Cards (Handysize → Supramax → Panamax → Capesize):

  • Point at the Progress Bar: “Look at this capacity bar. If we hire a Supramax for 50,000 tonnes, the bar is 85% Emerald Green (High efficiency). If we hire a giant Capesize for that small load, the bar turns Red (28%) because we’d be paying for empty space!”

2️⃣ Show the “Haldia Draft Trap” (Judges Love This!):

  • Action: In the Port dropdown, select “Haldia Dock Complex”.
  • Point at the Amber Alert that pops up: “Look at this warning! Haldia Port is only 8.5 meters deep. A big ship will get stuck in the mud! PortCast automatically adds the Sandheads lighterage barge fee (+$5.25/MT) and recommends Dhamra Port instead to save $180,000!”

🛰️ STEP 5: Click “Risk Radar” in Top Bar (#risk)

1️⃣ Read the World Map:

  • Point at Red Sea and Malacca Strait: “Our interactive map tracks critical maritime chokepoints with live threat rings.”

2️⃣ Read the Waterfall Step Chart:

  • Point at the bars: “Down here, our stress simulator shows: Base Freight ($18.40) + Fuel Shock ($2.10) + Port Delay ($1.80) = Total Risk Ceiling ($25.70/MT).”

🎯 STEP 6: Conclude (10 Seconds)

🗣️ Say:

“In summary, PortCast saves Indian energy and steel companies 14.2% on every voyage by predicting freight rates, timing fixtures, and picking the right vessel and port. Thank you!”
